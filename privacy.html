Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and share your information when you interact with our services and receive text communications from us.

Information We Collect
We collect personal information that you provide to us, such as your name, email address, and mobile phone number when you sign up for our services or request an appointment reminder.

How We Use Your Information
We use the information we collect to provide, maintain, and improve our services, including sending appointment notifications, event updates, and customer care responses.

SMS/Text Message Information & Non-Sharing Policy
No Mobile Information Sharing: We will never share, sell, rent, or trade your mobile phone number or opt-in consent data with any third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Any data collected through our SMS program is strictly used for internal communication, such as sending your requested appointment confirmations or event reminders. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

Message Frequency & Rates
Frequency: Message frequency varies based on your appointments and interactions. Typically, you will receive one message per appointment or event you sign up for.

Rates: Message and data rates may apply. Check with your mobile carrier for details on your messaging plan.

Opting Out
You can cancel the SMS service at any time. Just text "STOP" to the number you received the message from. Upon sending "STOP", we will send you an SMS reply to confirm that you have been unsubscribed. After this, you will no longer receive SMS messages from us.

Contact Us
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us.
